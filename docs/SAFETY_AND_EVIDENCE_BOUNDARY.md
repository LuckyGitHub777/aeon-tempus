# Safety and Evidence Boundary

AEON-TEMPUS / Theta-7 may be presented as speculative design, symbolic decision support, contemplative practice, narrative worldbuilding, creative research art, or an uncertainty-reflection interface.

It must not be represented as proof of a literal spacetime portal, extraterrestrial infrastructure, paranormal temporal mechanism, medical treatment, or scientifically demonstrated technology.

## Integrity rules
- Symbol is not proof.
- Intuition is not automatically evidence.
- Unknown information remains unknown until evidence supports a stronger conclusion.
- Human agency, consent, legality, dignity, and non-harm take priority.
- No dream, vision, system, transmission, or claimed entity overrides ethical judgment.
- The project does not authorize self-harm, harm to others, trespass, stalking, coercion, or dangerous experimentation.
- When an experience produces fear, disorientation, sleep disruption, or impaired functioning, grounding, trusted human support, and qualified professional care take priority.

## Privacy
The interface uses unencrypted browser localStorage. Do not enter secrets, passwords, classified information, protected medical information, confidential third-party material, or other highly sensitive data.

# Eclipse Node Theta-7 Engineering Specification

Theta-7 is the seventh threshold node in AEON-TEMPUS. It models a decision state where familiar illumination is obscured, uncertainty is explicit, and a human must act without manufacturing certainty.

## Governing equation
**OBSCURATION + DISCERNMENT + CHOICE = REVEALED DIRECTION**

## Seven chambers
1. **Arrival** — establish presence.
2. **Obscuration** — name what is unclear without inventing contents.
3. **Reflection** — distinguish directly observed, externally verified, remembered, inferred, imagined, symbolic, and unresolved material.
4. **Resonance** — examine meaning, emotion, hope/fear, and evidence that would change the interpretation.
5. **Reconciliation** — preserve, correct, leave uncertain, release, and identify what requires further observation.
6. **Selection** — choose one grounded action subject to non-harm, consent, reversibility, and preservation of responsible future options.
7. **Return** — retain the lesson, release unsupported certainty, and deliberately return to ordinary reality.

## Completion semantics
v1.1.1-rc1 records **self-attested sequence completion**, not proof that the interpretation is true or that the framework is effective. It requires substantive entries in critical reasoning fields plus the seven confirmations, action safeguards, and a grounding choice.
